About

Making software feel considered, not crowded.

I work with early teams and established studios to shape products people actually finish using. Most of my time goes to interface systems, prototyping in the browser, and writing the small bits of copy that make a screen feel human.

Before freelance work I designed tools at a logistics startup and a design studio. I still like the unglamorous problems: forms, empty states, and the screen someone opens at 11 p.m.

  • Interface design and design systems
  • HTML, CSS, and JavaScript prototypes
